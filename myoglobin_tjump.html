What is being measured?

At the selected wavelength near 220 nm, the native, helix-rich protein has a more negative CD signal. Unfolding makes the signal less negative; refolding reverses the direction. When the temperature is changed, the population begins to approach its new equilibrium.

For N ⇌ D, once the temperature has stabilized, krelax = ku + kf and CD(t) = A exp(−krelaxt) + B. Time in the downloaded CSV begins when the recording phase begins.
